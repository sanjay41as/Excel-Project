Saffron Spoon Sales Performance Analysis
1. Project Title
Sales Performance Analysis and Insights | January – August 2026
Dataset Description
This Excel dashboard analyzes sales performance for Saffron Spoon, a food and beverage business. The dashboard provides an interactive view of sales, orders, quantities, payment methods, products, managers, cities, and purchase methods to identify key sales trends and business insights.
The analysis focuses on sales transactions across different products, cities, managers, payment methods, and purchase channels.
________________________________________
2. Business Questions
The dashboard was designed to answer the following business questions:
1.	Which products contribute the most to total sales? 
2.	How does sales performance vary across different months? 
3.	Which payment method is most frequently used by customers? 
4.	Which managers and cities generate the highest sales? 
5.	Which purchase method contributes the highest sales and number of orders?

4. Key Findings
•	Burgers generated the highest sales among the displayed product categories, with approximately $376,658.04 in sales. 
•	Credit Card was the most frequently used payment method, accounting for approximately 47% of orders, followed by Cash at 30% and Gift Card at 23%. 
•	May recorded the highest monthly sales in the displayed period, at approximately $119,693.80. 
•	Among managers, Joao Silva recorded the highest sales at approximately $241,509.38. 
•	Lisbon generated the highest city-level sales, at approximately $241,509.38. 
•	Among purchase methods, Online recorded the highest number of orders, with 107 orders, while also showing the highest total sales among the three purchase methods displayed.
